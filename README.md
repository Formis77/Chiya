# Chiya 🍵

A Himalayan teahouse tycoon for the phone, inspired by grindy simulators like Bee Swarm Simulator.

Walk your fields, let your crew gather ingredients, brew them into chiya at your teahouse, and turn rupees into new crew, tools, dokos and higher fields.

- 23 crew types across 6 rarities, each drawn and animated, with unique abilities and VFX
- Blessed crew with teahouse-wide passives, levels via feeding, Mystic Wheel rerolls
- 14 synergies (see [SYNERGIES.md](SYNERGIES.md)) and 3 teahouse colors
- 9 fields from Tea Garden to Summit Snowfield, tools, dokos, boots, consumables, quests
- Works offline, saves automatically, and keeps earning while closed (lodge manager)

Play: open the GitHub Pages link in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
