## Teahouse colors

When at least half of your crew (8+ crew) share a color, your teahouse takes that color.

- 🔥 **Spice Teahouse**: Ability cooldowns −15%, +5% crit chance. Rewards active play.
- ❄️ **Ice Teahouse**: +30% rupees from brewing, +20% offline earnings. Great for idle days.
- ☁️ **Cloud Teahouse**: Your tool gathers ×1.5, +15% doko capacity. For hands-on farming.

Crew gather ×1.5 on tiles of their own color. Neutral crew get ×1.15 everywhere.
